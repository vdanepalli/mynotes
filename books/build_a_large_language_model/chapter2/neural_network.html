The Goal: Find weight $w$ and bias $b$ so that given input $x = 2.0$, the output equals target $y = 10.0$.

Geometric View: The Loss Bowl ($L$ vs $w$)

The red dot is your model. The dotted line is the slope (derivative $\frac{\partial L}{\partial w}$). Notice that a negative slope tilts down to the right—meaning you must increase $w$ to reach the valley!

Chain Rule Breakdown:
∂L/∂w = (∂L/∂ŷ) × (∂ŷ/∂w)
∂L/∂w = 2·(ŷ - y) × x
∂L/∂w = 2·(7.0 - 10.0) × 2.0 = -12.00
